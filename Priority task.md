# Priority task

## BEHOLD FIT — Content Priority Criterion

**Definition:**

A topic has strong Behold Fit when it closely matches the clients Yiya serves, the struggles she is qualified to treat, and the compassionate, trauma-informed approaches she actually uses.

### 1. Core specialties

Prioritize topics involving:

- Trauma, PTSD and attachment wounds
- Anxiety, overwhelm, stress and depression
- Relationship issues and family conflict
- Grief, breakup, divorce and major life transitions
- Childhood emotional wounds and unmet developmental needs
- Codependency, boundaries, anger and self-esteem
- Chronic illness, chronic pain and medical trauma
- ADHD and emotional regulation
- Racial, cultural and identity-related experiences
- Spiritual struggle, meaning and Christian-integrated healing when appropriate

### 2. Treatment approach

The topic should allow Behold to offer one or more of these treatment perspectives:

- **IFS:** Understanding and compassionately relating to protective, wounded or conflicted inner parts.
- **DNMS:** Healing attachment wounds and helping younger wounded parts receive unmet emotional and developmental needs.
- **EMDR:** Processing distressing memories and reducing the present-day impact of trauma.
- **Somatic therapy:** Noticing how stress and trauma are held in the body and supporting nervous-system regulation.
- **Mindfulness:** Developing nonjudgmental awareness of thoughts, emotions, sensations and behavioural patterns.
- **Attachment-based therapy:** Understanding how earlier relationships affect present trust, safety, boundaries and connection.
- **Culturally sensitive care:** Considering cultural, family, racial, social and environmental influences rather than locating the whole problem inside the individual.
- **Spiritually integrated care:** Making room for faith, spiritual struggle, meaning and hope when relevant to the client.

### 3. Desired client outcomes

Strong-fit content should help readers:

- Understand their emotional and bodily responses with less shame
- Calm and regulate their nervous system
- Recognize protective patterns and wounded parts
- Process grief or traumatic experiences
- Build self-awareness and self-compassion
- Clarify needs, values, boundaries and goals
- Reclaim agency and make healthier decisions
- Develop resilience, emotional freedom and more meaningful relationships

These criteria reflect the specialties and treatment approaches described in Yiya’s [Psychology Today profile](https://www.psychologytoday.com/ca/therapists/yiya-yin-trauma-attachment-emdr-slidingscale-vancouver-bc/831616?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

## Simple 1–5 scoring scale

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| **5 — Excellent fit** | Directly addresses a core Behold specialty, clearly connects with IFS, DNMS, EMDR, somatic or mindfulness work, and would be highly useful to current or prospective clients. |
| **4 — Strong fit** | Closely related to Behold’s specialties and treatment approach, although not central to both. |
| **3 — Moderate fit** | Relevant to general emotional wellness, but the connection to Behold’s specialized trauma and attachment work is limited. |
| **2 — Weak fit** | Only loosely related to Behold’s clients or requires expertise outside the primary practice focus. |
| **1 — Not a fit** | Primarily medical, diagnostic, legal or unrelated to Behold’s counselling expertise and treatment approach. |

### Freelancer shortcut

Ask these three questions:

1. **Is this a struggle Behold’s clients commonly bring to counselling?**
2. **Can Yiya address it meaningfully through IFS, DNMS, EMDR, somatic or mindfulness-informed work?**
3. **Would the resource help readers gain understanding, regulation, self-compassion or agency?**

If all three answers are **yes**, the topic has strong Behold Fit.

**Example—score 5:**

“Why do I shut down during conflict even when I want to communicate?”

It fits attachment trauma and relationship issues; it can be explored through protective parts, nervous-system responses, somatic awareness and trauma processing; and it gives readers a compassionate explanation plus a path toward regulation and agency.
