# First Task

Here is a simple glossary 

| Term | Simple definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content opportunity** | A useful question or problem that Behold could address through an article or resource. | Many women search for why they feel emotionally numb after prolonged stress. |
| **Working title** | A temporary title describing what the content will cover. | “Why Do I Feel Numb After Years of Holding Everything Together?” |
| **Intended audience** | The specific people the resource is meant to help. | Spiritually sensitive women aged 35–65 experiencing emotional exhaustion. |
| **Client need** | The emotional or practical problem behind the search. | The person wants to understand whether emotional numbness means something is wrong with them. |
| **Search intent** | What the person hopes to find or accomplish when searching. | Someone searching “why do I feel emotionally numb?” wants understanding and possible next steps—not immediately a sales page. |
| **Demand signal** | Visible evidence that people are asking about or engaging with a topic. | The same question appears in Google’s “People also ask,” several Reddit discussions and popular therapist videos. |
| **Google autocomplete** | Suggestions Google displays while someone types a search. | Typing “why do I feel numb…” produces “why do I feel numb after trauma?” |
| **People Also Ask** | Related questions Google displays inside search results. | “Can your nervous system shut down after prolonged stress?” |
| **Audience language** | The words real people use to describe their experiences. | “I’m functioning, but I don’t feel anything anymore.” |
| **Source link** | The direct webpage where the information or demand signal was found. | A link to a research paper, professional organization or relevant discussion. |
| **Credible source** | A trustworthy source with qualified authorship and reliable evidence. | The American Psychological Association, a peer-reviewed journal or a recognized trauma researcher. |
| **Peer-reviewed research** | Research evaluated by other qualified scholars before publication. | A journal article examining emotional numbing following traumatic stress. |
| **Professional organization** | A recognized clinical, health or counselling association. | BC Association of Clinical Counsellors or the Canadian Psychological Association. |
| **Audience-language source** | A discussion showing how people describe a problem; it is not treated as clinical evidence. | A Reddit thread where women describe feeling numb after years of caregiving. |
| **Evidence map** | An organized list showing which sources support each part of the future article. | Research source for nervous-system shutdown; clinical source for symptoms; audience source for lived-experience language. |
| **Unique Behold angle** | The distinct perspective Behold brings to a familiar subject. | Explaining numbness compassionately as protection while including body awareness, relational healing and spiritual sensitivity. |
| **Relevance to Behold** | Why the topic fits Behold’s clients, expertise and services. | The topic connects trauma recovery with the experiences of emotionally exhausted, spiritually sensitive women. |
| **Ranked opportunity** | A topic placed in order of value compared with the other topics. | Topic #1 has strong demand, fits Behold’s expertise and can lead naturally to counselling; Topic #10 has weaker evidence of interest. |
| **Ranking criteria** | The standards used to decide which topics are strongest. | Audience need, evidence of interest, relevance to Behold, quality of available evidence and usefulness to clients. |
| **Search method** | A record of exactly how the research was conducted. | Searched Google for “emotional numbness after prolonged stress,” reviewed People Also Ask, then checked Reddit and YouTube. |
| **AI-assisted material** | Research, summaries or ideas created partly with an AI tool. | “ChatGPT helped group the questions, but I manually verified every source and citation.” |
| **Manually verified citation** | A source the freelancer personally opened and checked. | Confirming that the paper exists, the authors are correct and it genuinely supports the claim. |
| **Deliverable** | The completed item the freelancer must submit. | One Google Doc containing ten opportunities, one recommendation, an evidence map and the research process. |
| **Completion criteria** | The checklist used to determine whether the assignment is finished. | Ten documented opportunities are included, every link works and the freelancer reports the hours and final cost. |

### Example of one completed opportunity

**Working title:** Why Do I Feel Emotionally Numb When My Life Looks Fine?

**Audience:** High-functioning women who appear capable but feel disconnected internally.

**Client need:** Reassurance and an understandable explanation of emotional numbness.

**Search intent:** To understand the cause of the experience and learn what might help.

**Demand signals:** Google questions, several Reddit discussions and therapist videos addressing emotional numbness.

**Behold angle:** Emotional numbness is approached compassionately as a protective response—not a personal failure—with attention to the body, relationships and spiritual life.

**Why it fits:** It supports Behold’s trauma-informed work and could become a resource shared with clients who struggle to describe this experience.

To simplify and streamline the process while keeping relevance, what 3-5 metric would we use in priority

Use these five priority metrics. Score each topic from **1–5**, then total the scores out of **25**.

| Priority metric | Simple question | What a 5 means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Audience relevance** | Is this a real and important struggle for Behold’s ideal clients? | Closely matches emotionally exhausted, spiritually sensitive adults seeking trauma-informed support. |
| **2. Visible demand** | Is there documented evidence that people are asking about it? | Appears repeatedly across Google questions, Reddit discussions or therapist YouTube content. |
| **3. Behold fit** | Does this connect strongly with your expertise and approach? | Strong fit with trauma, grief, relational healing, nervous-system regulation or spiritually integrated counselling. |
| **4. Client usefulness** | Would this resource help someone understand themselves or take a safe next step? | Immediately useful as an educational resource, FAQ or something you could send to clients instead of repeatedly explaining it. |
| **5. Distinctive angle** | Can Behold contribute something more thoughtful than existing generic articles? | Clear opportunity for your compassionate, spiritually sensitive and relational perspective. |

### Priority rule

- **21–25:** Create first
- **17–20:** Strong future topic
- **13–16:** Needs a sharper angle
- **12 or lower:** Do not prioritize

If you want maximum simplicity, use only the first three: **Audience relevance + Visible demand + Behold fit**. These determine whether the topic is needed, discoverable and appropriate for your practice.
